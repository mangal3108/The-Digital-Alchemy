## 1. Compliance & Usage Principles

All logo usage across the site adheres to strict trademark and brand compliance standards:
1. **Genuine Official Marks Only**: Sourced directly from official corporate brand resource kits, verified SVG mirrors (Simple Icons), or official developer registries (Devicon). No redrawn, AI-generated, or CSS-recreated marks.
2. **Neutral Phrasing (No Implied Endorsements)**: Headings and section titles use neutral descriptive copy ("Platforms we work with", "Technologies we build on", "Tools we use", "Campaign channels"). **No** "Google Partner", "Meta Partner", "Shopify Partner", or "AWS Partner" badges unless verified and confirmed by the business owner.
3. **Trademark Protection**: Clean space and minimum dimensions are strictly maintained per each brand's official brand guidelines. No stretching, hue rotation, recoloring, or forbidden background contrast.
4. **Isolated Page UI**: Logos live strictly as standalone, semantic, crisp SVG elements in page layout strips or feature grids with text labels. Never embedded into AI hero images.
5. **Client Logos**: Completely excluded until explicit written release/whitelist is provided.
